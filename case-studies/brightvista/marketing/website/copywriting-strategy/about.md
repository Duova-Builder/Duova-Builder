## About BrightVista

### Professional Exterior Home Improvement & Maintenance

BrightVista Home Services Ltd. provides reliable exterior home improvement and maintenance services for homeowners throughout Greater Vancouver. From refreshing the exterior of your home to improving your outdoor living space, we help keep residential properties clean, attractive, and well maintained.
Our services include exterior house painting, roof soft washing, exterior wall and driveway pressure washing, fence replacement, yard landscaping, and aluminum pergola installation. Whether your property needs seasonal maintenance, exterior improvements, or a complete outdoor refresh, our team provides practical solutions tailored to your project.
We focus on quality workmanship, clear communication, and dependable service. With experienced workers, quality materials, and professional equipment, BrightVista is committed to delivering clean, professional results while making the process straightforward for homeowners.
Serving Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, Coquitlam, Langley, and North Vancouver, BrightVista Home Services Ltd. is here to help homeowners maintain and improve the exterior of their properties.
