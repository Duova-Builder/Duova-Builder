# Website Copywriting Strategy

## BrightVista Home Services Ltd.

### 1. Overall Copywriting Strategy

The website copy is designed around three primary goals:

1. Clearly communicate BrightVista's core residential exterior services.
2. Build trust with homeowners who are comparing local contractors.
3. Improve organic search visibility across Greater Vancouver.
   The copy intentionally combines **service keywords, location keywords, customer benefits, and trust signals** rather than repeatedly inserting keywords into every paragraph.
   The primary positioning is:
   **Residential Exterior Home Improvement & Maintenance Contractor in Greater Vancouver**
   This positioning connects the six individual services under one broader service category instead of presenting the company as an unrelated collection of services.

### 2. Hero Section Strategy

**Hero Title**
Professional Exterior Home Services in Greater Vancouver
**Subtitle**
Reliable exterior painting, soft washing, pressure washing, fencing, landscaping, and pergola installation for homeowners . 
**CTA**
Get a free quote today!

### 3. About Section Strategy

The About section establishes the company as a:
**Residential exterior maintenance and improvement provider**
rather than simply a general contractor.
The copy naturally incorporates:

* residential properties
* exterior house painting
* roof soft washing
* pressure washing
* fence replacement
* landscaping
* aluminum pergola installation
* Greater Vancouver
  This creates topical relevance while keeping the text readable.
  The section also introduces trust signals:
* experienced workers
* quality materials
* professional equipment
* clear communication
* dependable service
* attention to detail
  These concepts are important because homeowners are not only searching for a service; they are evaluating whether they can trust the contractor entering their property.

### 4. Service Page / Service Section Strategy

Each service follows the same structure:
**Service Name → Benefit-Oriented Subtitle → Description → Service Highlights**
This creates consistency across the website and makes each service easy for both users and search engines to understand.
For example:
**Exterior House Painting**
is the primary service keyword, while:
**Refresh Your Home's Exterior**
communicates the customer benefit.
The description then provides supporting topical terms such as:

* exterior walls
* siding
* surface preparation
* paint application
* residential exterior
  This approach provides semantic context without excessive repetition of the exact keyword.

### 5. Local SEO Strategy

The website targets the broader **Greater Vancouver** market while explicitly identifying the main service areas:

* Vancouver
* Burnaby
* Richmond
* Surrey
* Coquitlam
* Langley
* North Vancouver
  These locations are included naturally in the homepage and service-area section.
  The objective is not to repeatedly place every city name into every paragraph. Excessive repetition would make the copy unnatural and could create a poor user experience.
  Instead, location relevance is distributed across:
* Hero section
* About section
* Service Area section
* Individual service pages
* LocalBusiness / service-area structured data where appropriate

### 6. Conversion Strategy

SEO traffic is only valuable when visitors can understand the business and take action.
The homepage therefore uses several conversion points:

* Get a Free Quote
* Explore Our Services
* Learn More About BrightVista
* Contact information
* Customer reviews
  The copy moves the visitor through a simple sequence:
  **What do you do? → Can you help me? → Why should I trust you? → Where do you work? → How do I contact you?**

### 7. Trust Signal Strategy

The “Why Choose BrightVista?” section uses six practical trust signals:

1. Experienced Professionals
2. Quality Materials
3. Professional Equipment
4. Quick Response
5. Timely Service
6. Workmanship & After-Service Support
   These were selected because contractor customers commonly care about workmanship, reliability, communication, materials, scheduling, and post-project support.
   Only claims that can be verified by the business should be published.
   For example, warranty wording should match the company's actual warranty terms.

### 8. Review Strategy

The heading:
**“People Say the Nicest Things”**
creates a friendly transition into social proof.
However, customer reviews should always be genuine and verifiable.
The website should never use invented testimonials simply to make the company appear more established.
When real reviews become available, they can be added together with the customer's name and location where appropriate.

### 9. Keyword Strategy

The website's primary keyword themes are:
**Core**

* exterior home services
* exterior home improvement
* home exterior maintenance
* residential exterior services
  **Service**
* exterior house painting
* roof soft washing
* pressure washing
* driveway pressure washing
* fence replacement
* yard landscaping
* aluminum pergola installation
  **Local**
* Greater Vancouver
* Vancouver
* Burnaby
* Richmond
* Surrey
* Coquitlam
* Langley
* North Vancouver
  The strategy is based on **topic coverage and search intent**, not keyword density.
  The goal is to make each page clearly relevant to a specific service and geographic market.

### 10. Content Architecture

The homepage introduces the complete service portfolio.
Each major service should eventually have its own dedicated page:

* `/exterior-house-painting/`
* `/roof-soft-washing/`
* `/pressure-washing/`
* `/fence-replacement/`
* `/yard-landscaping/`
* `/aluminum-pergola-installation/`
  Dedicated service pages provide significantly more room to target specific search intent than putting every keyword onto the homepage.
  The homepage acts as the central hub and links to the individual service pages.

### 11. Design Principle

The copy follows a simple principle:
**SEO should support the customer experience, not replace it.**
The visitor should understand the company within a few seconds, find the service they need quickly, see evidence that the company is reliable, and have an obvious way to request a quote.
Search engines should be able to understand the same information through clear headings, service-specific content, geographic relevance, internal links, metadata, and structured data.
