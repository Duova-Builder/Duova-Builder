# Google Business Profile Description Strategy

## Purpose

The Google Business Profile description is designed to help Google understand the business type, service offerings, and target service area. The goal is to improve relevance for local searches while keeping the description natural and professional.

## Keyword Strategy

The description includes important local SEO keywords related to the business:

### Business Type Keywords

* home improvement contractor
* exterior maintenance
* residential properties

These keywords help Google identify BrightVista Home Services Ltd. as a contractor providing home improvement and exterior services.

### Service Keywords

The main services are included naturally:

* exterior house painting
* roof soft washing
* pressure washing
* driveway cleaning
* fence replacement
* landscaping
* aluminum gazebo installation

These match the search terms potential customers may use when looking for specific services.

### Location Keywords

The service area is included to improve local relevance:

* Vancouver
* Burnaby
* Richmond
* Surrey
* Coquitlam
* Langley
* North Vancouver
* Greater Vancouver

Including multiple nearby cities helps Google associate the business with searches from different areas in Metro Vancouver.

## Writing Approach

The description avoids keyword stuffing and does not repeat location or service keywords unnaturally. Google evaluates the overall business profile, including categories, services, reviews, photos, and customer activity. The description provides additional context rather than trying to rank through keywords alone.

## Structure

1. Opening sentence:

   * Clearly explains the business type and service area.
   * Includes the main local SEO signal.

2. Service section:

   * Lists the core services customers are searching for.
   * Helps Google match the profile with relevant queries.

3. Trust section:

   * Highlights workmanship, communication, and quality.
   * Makes the description more customer-focused instead of only keyword-focused.

## Future Optimization

The description can be updated later based on:

* Google Search Console search terms
* Google Business Profile performance data
* Customer review keywords
* New services added by the company

The strongest local ranking factors will continue to be:

* Accurate primary and secondary categories
* Complete service list
* Consistent business information (NAP)
* Customer reviews
* Photos and project updates
* Regular Google Business Profile activity
