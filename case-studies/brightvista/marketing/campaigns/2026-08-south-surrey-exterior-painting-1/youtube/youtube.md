A great exterior paint job starts long before the first coat of paint goes on.

For this South Surrey exterior painting project, our team focused heavily on preparation and protection. Before painting, the areas that needed to remain clean and protected were carefully covered, masked, and secured—including windows, doors, surrounding surfaces, and other areas around the work zone.

This preparation may not be the most visible part of a painting project, but it makes a big difference. Proper masking helps prevent unwanted paint splatter, keeps surrounding surfaces clean, and allows our team to work more precisely around edges, trim, windows, and other details.

💡 Exterior Painting Tip:

Peeling, cracking, or faded paint is often more than just a cosmetic issue. When exterior surfaces are not properly prepared, new paint may have difficulty bonding to the surface, which can lead to premature peeling, cracking, or uneven results.

That’s why proper preparation is an important part of a durable exterior paint job. Cleaning, surface preparation, repairs where needed, masking, and protecting surrounding areas all help create a better foundation for the finish coat.

For homes in Metro Vancouver, preparation is especially important because exterior surfaces are regularly exposed to rain, moisture, temperature changes, and seasonal weather.

A quality paint job isn't just about applying paint—it’s about taking care of the details before, during, and after the painting process.
