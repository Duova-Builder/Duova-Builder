🏡 South Surrey Exterior Painting Project

The finished paint job is what you see—but the preparation is what helps make the finish last.

For this South Surrey home, we took extra care before painting to protect and mask the areas that needed to stay clean.

Windows, doors, surrounding surfaces, and other areas around the work zone were carefully covered and protected before the painting began.

Why does this matter?
🎨 Proper masking helps prevent unwanted paint splatter and keeps surrounding surfaces clean.
🛠️ Careful preparation helps paint adhere properly to the surface.
🏠 Surface preparation can help reduce premature peeling, cracking, and uneven finishes.

🌧️ This is especially important in Metro Vancouver, where exterior surfaces are exposed to frequent rain and moisture.

In this project video, you’ll see the preparation, work in progress, and the completed exterior.

A quality exterior painting project isn't simply about putting paint on a wall. The details before the first coat are just as important as the final coat.
