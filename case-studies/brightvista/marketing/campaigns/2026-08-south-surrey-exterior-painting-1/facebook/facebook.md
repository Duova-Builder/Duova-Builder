🏡 South Surrey Exterior House Painting Project | Preparation Matters

Here’s a look at one of our exterior house painting projects in South Surrey, BC.

Before applying paint, our team carefully prepared the work area and protected the surfaces that needed to remain clean. Windows, doors, surrounding areas, and other surfaces were properly covered and masked before painting began.

This step is easy to overlook, but careful preparation is an important part of a quality exterior painting project.
Why?
✔️ Helps prevent paint from getting onto unwanted surfaces
✔️ Creates cleaner, more precise edges
✔️ Helps the painting team work more efficiently
✔️ Gives the new coating a properly prepared surface to bond to
✔️ Helps reduce problems such as premature peeling and uneven coverage

Exterior paint also has to deal with much more than appearance. In the Greater Vancouver area, homes are exposed to rain, moisture, seasonal temperature changes, and other outdoor conditions. Proper preparation can make a significant difference in how the finished coating performs over time.
