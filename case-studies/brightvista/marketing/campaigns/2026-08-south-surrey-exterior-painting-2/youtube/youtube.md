🏡 Before the Paint Goes On: Why Preparation Matters

A beautiful exterior finish doesn't start with the paint—it starts with the preparation.

For this Vancouver home, the project included several preparation steps before the final finish:
🧰 Surface cleaning and preparation
🪵 Removing loose or deteriorated material
🔧 Preparing surfaces for proper paint adhesion
🏠 Protecting surrounding areas
🎨 Applying the final finish

Why is this important?
Exterior paint does more than change the color of your home. It also helps protect exterior surfaces from moisture, weather, and everyday wear.

In Vancouver's wet climate, poorly prepared surfaces can contribute to premature peeling, cracking, and uneven paint coverage.

That's why proper preparation is one of the most important parts of a lasting exterior paint project.
