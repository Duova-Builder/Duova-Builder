💡 Did you know?
Pressure washing is not simply about turning up the pressure. Different surfaces require different cleaning approaches. Concrete and other durable hard surfaces can generally tolerate more pressure, while wood and more delicate materials may require a gentler approach. Choosing the right technique helps achieve a clean result while reducing the risk of surface damage.
