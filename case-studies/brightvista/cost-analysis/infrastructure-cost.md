Why This Plan
* The one-year plan was selected because it keeps the upfront investment lower while providing all the features required for a newly launched business website.
* The hosting plan can be upgraded later if additional storage or performance becomes necessary.

The selected Hostinger Premium plan includes:
* 20 GB SSD storage
* Managed WordPress hosting
* One-click WordPress installation
* Weekly automatic backups
* Free SSL certificate
* Automatic SSL renewal
* Up to two business email accounts for the first year
* Upgrade path for future growth

# Business Email

Hostinger includes two business email accounts for the first year.

Originally, the plan was to create professional email addresses such as:

* support@brightvista.ca
* info@brightvista.ca

However, the client currently manages all business communications through an existing email account and preferred not to change this workflow immediately to avoid disrupting ongoing operations.

This can be introduced later without changing the website infrastructure.

# Overall Strategy

The infrastructure was selected based on long-term value rather than the lowest first-year cost.

The combination of Hostinger and Cloudflare provides:
* Affordable operating costs
* Reliable hosting
* Professional domain management
* Free SSL
* Free DNS
* Automatic backups
* Room for future growth

This creates a stable and cost-effective foundation for a local business website while keeping future maintenance simple.
