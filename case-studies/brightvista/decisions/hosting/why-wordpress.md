# Why We Chose WordPress for This Project

## Project Background

BrightVista Home Services Ltd. is a local home services company based in Vancouver, Canada. The company provides exterior home maintenance services, including:

* Exterior house painting
* Roof soft washing
* Pressure washing
* Fence replacement
* Landscaping
* Aluminum pergola installation

Unlike an e-commerce business, BrightVista does not sell products online. Its primary objective is to generate qualified local leads and establish trust with homeowners searching for reliable contractors.

The website is expected to become the company's digital storefront and serve as the center of its online presence across multiple platforms, including:

* Google Business Profile
* Instagram
* Facebook
* X (Twitter)
* TikTok
* Xiaohongshu
* Yelp

The business owner is not a developer and would like the ability to update photos, videos, and service information without writing code.

Therefore, the platform needed to satisfy the following requirements:

* Professional business presentation
* Easy content management
* Strong Local SEO capabilities
* Fast loading speed
* High lead conversion potential
* Long-term scalability
* Reasonable operating cost
* Full ownership of business data

---

# Evaluating the Available Options

Rather than choosing a website platform based on popularity, each option was evaluated against the actual business requirements.

## Option 1: Durable

Durable is designed around one idea: speed.

A complete website can be generated in minutes using AI, making it an excellent solution for businesses that simply need an online presence as quickly as possible.

### Advantages

* Extremely fast deployment
* Almost no learning curve
* AI-generated copy and layouts
* Built-in CRM and simple business tools
* Suitable for validating a new business

### Limitations

The simplicity that makes Durable attractive also limits its long-term potential.

Customization is minimal, SEO controls are basic, and content management is relatively limited.

For a business planning to continuously publish projects, improve search rankings, and grow its online visibility, Durable becomes restrictive.

**Conclusion**

Durable is an excellent choice for launching quickly, but not for building a long-term digital marketing foundation.

---

## Option 2: Wix

Wix focuses on visual design.

Its drag-and-drop editor gives users tremendous creative freedom without requiring programming knowledge.

### Advantages

* Beautiful visual editor
* Easy for beginners
* Fully managed hosting
* Good built-in business tools
* Improved SEO compared with previous generations

### Limitations

Although Wix has matured significantly, it remains a closed SaaS platform.

Migrating away from Wix in the future can be difficult, and developers have limited control over the underlying architecture.

For businesses that expect continuous expansion, advanced SEO strategies, or custom integrations, Wix eventually reaches its ceiling.

**Conclusion**

Wix is an excellent platform for portfolios, restaurants, photographers, and visually driven brands, but it was not the best long-term fit for this project.

---

## Option 3: Shopify

Shopify is one of the world's best e-commerce platforms.

However, BrightVista is not an online store.

The company does not manage inventory, process product orders, or operate a shopping cart.

### Advantages

* Industry-leading checkout experience
* Excellent inventory management
* Powerful payment ecosystem
* Outstanding scalability for online retail

### Limitations

Most of Shopify's strengths revolve around selling products.

Using Shopify for a local service company would introduce unnecessary complexity and ongoing subscription costs without providing meaningful business value.

The company simply doesn't need an industrial-grade commerce platform.

**Conclusion**

Shopify solves the wrong problem for this business.

---

## Option 4: WordPress

WordPress offers the greatest flexibility among all evaluated platforms.

Unlike closed SaaS solutions, WordPress gives businesses complete ownership of their website, content, and data.

Although it requires more technical setup initially, it provides the strongest long-term foundation.

### Advantages

* Full ownership of data
* Excellent Local SEO capabilities
* Industry-leading content management
* Massive plugin ecosystem
* Flexible website structure
* Easy media management
* Cost-effective hosting options
* Unlimited customization possibilities

Most importantly, after the initial setup, the business owner can easily upload new project photos, videos, and service updates through the WordPress dashboard without touching any code.

This aligns perfectly with the client's long-term operational needs.

### Challenges

WordPress does require:

* Hosting management
* Security updates
* Plugin maintenance
* Regular backups

These responsibilities increase technical complexity compared with fully managed platforms.

However, once the website is properly configured with reliable hosting, automatic backups, and essential security plugins, the day-to-day management becomes straightforward for non-technical users.

---

# Why WordPress Was the Right Choice

The decision was ultimately driven by business requirements rather than technical preferences.

BrightVista needs a website that will continue growing over the coming years instead of simply existing online.

The website should:

* Rank in local Google searches
* Showcase completed projects
* Build trust through case studies and customer reviews
* Integrate naturally with Google Business Profile
* Support ongoing content marketing
* Generate qualified leads
* Allow the business owner to update content independently

WordPress meets all of these requirements while providing complete ownership of the company's digital assets.

Although the initial setup requires more technical expertise, the long-term benefits significantly outweigh the additional implementation effort.

---

# Final Decision

Every platform has its ideal use case.

* **Durable** is ideal for launching a simple service website in hours.
* **Wix** is an excellent choice for visually focused businesses that prioritize design simplicity.
* **Shopify** is the clear winner for e-commerce businesses.
* **WordPress** is the strongest option for businesses that value content, SEO, flexibility, and long-term growth.

For BrightVista Home Services Ltd., the objective was not merely to build a website.

The objective was to build a sustainable digital presence capable of attracting local customers, supporting future marketing efforts, and growing alongside the business.

For those reasons, WordPress was the most appropriate platform for this project.
