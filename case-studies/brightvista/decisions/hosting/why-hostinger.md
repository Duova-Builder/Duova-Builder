# Why We Chose Hostinger

## Business Requirements

BrightVista Home Services Ltd. is a local home services business, not a technology company.

The primary objective of this project was to build a professional online presence that could generate leads while keeping long-term operating costs reasonable.

The business owner has limited technical experience and prefers to invest in activities that directly contribute to business growth, such as improving the website, showcasing completed projects, and attracting new customers, rather than spending additional money on technical maintenance.

The hosting platform therefore needed to provide:

* Reliable uptime
* Good performance for visitors in British Columbia
* Automatic backups
* Free SSL
* Easy WordPress management
* Low operating cost
* Simple future maintenance
* A clear upgrade path as the business grows

---

# Option Considered: WordPress.com

WordPress.com was initially one of the strongest candidates.

Because it is operated by the company behind WordPress, it provides an excellent managed hosting experience with strong reliability, automatic updates, and a polished administration interface.

Initially, the Business plan was recommended because it includes automatic backups and additional management features, reducing the amount of technical work required after launch.

However, pricing became the main concern.

The lower-tier plan was more affordable but did not include automatic backups.

Since the client is not familiar with website management, relying on manual backups would eventually require developer assistance, increasing long-term maintenance costs.

The Business plan solved these issues but significantly increased the annual hosting cost.

Although it is an excellent platform, the additional expense was difficult to justify for a newly launched local business website.

---

# Why Hostinger

After comparing several hosting providers, Hostinger Premium offered the best balance between functionality, reliability, and cost.

The selected plan includes:

* 20 GB SSD storage
* Weekly automatic backups
* Free SSL certificate
* Managed WordPress hosting
* One-click WordPress installation
* Up to two free business email accounts for one year
* Easy-to-use hosting control panel
* Lower monthly operating cost
* Upgrade options as the business grows

For a newly launched local business website, 20 GB of storage is more than sufficient for service pages, project galleries, images, videos, and future content updates.

The included weekly backups reduce operational risk without requiring additional maintenance from either the client or the developer.

The free SSL certificate was another important factor.

SSL encrypts communication between visitors and the website, protecting information submitted through contact forms while enabling HTTPS across the entire website.

HTTPS has become an industry standard, improves customer confidence, and is considered a positive ranking signal by search engines.

Hostinger also manages SSL renewal automatically, eliminating another recurring maintenance task and reducing the risk of certificate expiration.

---

# Server Location

One consideration during the evaluation was server location.

Hostinger does not currently provide Canadian data centers.

The nearest available server is located in the United States.

Since BrightVista primarily serves customers in British Columbia, this introduces only a small amount of additional latency that is unlikely to be noticeable for normal website visitors.

Considering the overall pricing and feature set, the performance remains well within an acceptable range for this project.

---

# Business Email

The selected hosting plan also includes up to two free business email accounts for the first year.

Initially, the plan was to create professional email addresses such as:

* [support@brightvista.ca](mailto:support@brightvista.ca)
* [info@brightvista.ca](mailto:info@brightvista.ca)

Using a branded email address generally improves professionalism and customer trust.

However, the client currently manages all business communication through an existing email account and preferred not to change this workflow immediately to avoid disrupting ongoing operations.

As the business grows, branded email addresses can be introduced without requiring any changes to the hosting infrastructure.

---

# Future Scalability

The current website is relatively lightweight.

Its primary functions include:

* Company introduction
* Service pages
* Project galleries
* Contact forms
* Customer inquiries

The selected hosting plan comfortably supports these requirements.

If website traffic increases significantly in the future, Hostinger provides straightforward upgrade options without requiring the website to be rebuilt or migrated.

This allows the infrastructure to grow alongside the business while avoiding unnecessary upfront expenses.

# Final Decision

The objective of this project was not to purchase the most expensive hosting solution, but to select the platform that best matched the client's current business stage.

While WordPress.com provides an excellent fully managed experience, Hostinger Premium delivers the essential features required for a professional local business website at a significantly lower cost.

The combination of automatic backups, free SSL, straightforward WordPress management, reasonable pricing, and future scalability made Hostinger the most practical and cost-effective hosting solution for this project.
