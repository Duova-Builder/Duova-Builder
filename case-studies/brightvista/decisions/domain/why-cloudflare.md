# Why We Chose Cloudflare

## Business Requirements

A domain name is a long-term business asset.

The client wanted a professional domain while keeping future maintenance simple and affordable.

The DNS provider also needed to be reliable and easy to manage.

## Domain Registration
Hostinger includes a free domain for the first year, which initially appeared to be an attractive cost-saving option.

The ideal choice for the business was brightvista.com—it is concise, memorable, and carries the universally recognized .com extension. However, brightvista.com was already registered by another party and unavailable.

As the next best option, brightvista.ca was selected as the cleanest and most professional alternative. Unfortunately, this domain was not included in Hostinger's free domain promotion.

Hostinger did offer free registration for long-tail alternatives such as brightvistabc.com for the first year. However, compromising on the domain name introduced several long-term disadvantages:

* Less memorable and clean
* Less aligned with the core brand
* Higher renewal costs later
* Potential rebranding or migration work in the future

Instead of settling for an awkward domain name just to save on first-year costs, the decision prioritized long-term brand integrity.

## Why Cloudflare
The domain was registered directly through Cloudflare.

This provides several advantages.
### Transparent Pricing
Cloudflare charges domains at approximately wholesale cost without aggressive renewal markups. This reduces long-term ownership costs.

### Excellent DNS Management
Cloudflare offers one of the industry's fastest and most reliable DNS infrastructures.

Benefits include:
* Fast global DNS resolution
* High availability
* Easy record management
* Strong developer tools

### Security
Even on the free plan, Cloudflare provides useful security features such as:
* SSL management
* Basic DDoS protection
* DNS security
* Performance optimization

These features improve both reliability and user trust.

### Flexibility
Separating domain registration from website hosting also reduces vendor lock-in.

If the hosting provider changes in the future, the domain remains independent and migration becomes much simpler.

## Final Decision
Although brightvista.com was unavailable and taking Hostinger's free alternative (brightvistabc.com) would have reduced first-year costs, choosing brightvista.ca through Cloudflare created a much stronger long-term foundation.

The decision prioritized branding, maintainability, and operational simplicity over short-term savings.
