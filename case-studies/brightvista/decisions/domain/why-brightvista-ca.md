# Domain Selection

## Candidate Domains

Several domain options were evaluated during the planning stage.

### Option 1

**brightvista.com**

The ideal domain because it exactly matches the company name.

Unfortunately, it had already been registered.

### Option 2

**brightvistabc.com**

This domain qualified for Hostinger's free first-year promotion.

However, it introduced several drawbacks.

* Longer to type
* Less memorable
* Weaker branding
* Higher renewal costs after the promotional period

Although it reduced the initial cost, it was not considered the best long-term choice.

### Option 3

**brightvista.ca**

This domain clearly communicates that the business operates in Canada.

For a local home services company serving customers in British Columbia, the country-code top-level domain also reinforces local trust.

Although it required purchasing separately, the additional cost was relatively small compared with the long-term branding benefits.

## Final Decision

The website was launched using **brightvista.ca**.

The decision was based on branding rather than short-term discounts.

A domain name represents the business identity for many years.

Saving a small amount during the first year was not worth compromising the company's long-term online brand.
