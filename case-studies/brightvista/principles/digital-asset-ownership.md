# Principle: The Client Owns Their Digital Assets

At Dova Builder, every digital asset should be owned and controlled by the client from day one.
This includes, but is not limited to:

* Domain names
* Website hosting accounts
* Google Business Profile
* Google Search Console
* Google Analytics
* Social media accounts
* Business email accounts
* Other third-party platforms and services
  Whenever possible, all accounts are registered using the client's own email address, with Dova Builder added as an administrator, manager, or collaborator when necessary.

## Why this principle matters

### 1. The business owns its brand

A website, domain name, customer reviews, search rankings, analytics, and social media audiences are valuable business assets. These assets should remain with the business regardless of who builds or manages them.

### 2. No vendor lock-in

If every account is created under the developer's personal email, transferring ownership later often requires additional administrative work and may involve platform-specific restrictions. By keeping ownership with the client from the beginning, the business remains independent and can change agencies or service providers at any time.

### 3. Better security and compliance

Using the client's own accounts reduces unnecessary sharing of passwords and sensitive credentials. Access can be granted through role-based permissions instead of transferring account ownership.

### 4. Long-term continuity

Businesses may work with different designers, developers, or marketing agencies over time. Centralizing ownership under the company's own accounts ensures continuity and prevents the loss of important data or access.

## Our role

Dova Builder builds, configures, and manages digital platforms on behalf of clients, but we do not own their digital assets. Our goal is to help businesses establish a secure, sustainable, and transferable digital foundation that they fully control.
