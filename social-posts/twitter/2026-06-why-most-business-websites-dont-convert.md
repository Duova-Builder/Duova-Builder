Most business websites don't have a traffic problem.
They have a conversion problem.
I've reviewed hundreds of business websites.
Most look great.
Almost none are built to generate leads.
Here are the 8 biggest mistakes I see (and how to fix them):

1. Slow websites kill revenue.
Every extra second costs you visitors.
✓ <2s load time
✓ CDN
✓ WebP images
✓ Lazy loading
✓ Server close to your customers
Speed is a business metric.
Not just a technical one.

2. Your homepage has 3 seconds.
Visitors should instantly know:
• Who you are
• What you do
• Who you help
• Why they should trust you
Confused visitors never convert.

3. Every page needs ONE next step.
Don't make people think.
Use clear CTAs:
→ Get a Quote
→ Book a Demo
→ Contact Sales
The easier the action...
the higher the conversion.

4. Mobile first. Always.
Over 70% of visitors are on mobile.
Test your own website on your phone.
You'll find more problems in 5 minutes than most agencies find in a week.

5. People don't buy websites.
They buy trust.
Show:
✓ Customer reviews
✓ Case studies
✓ Team photos
✓ Client logos
✓ Certifications
Stock photos don't build credibility.
Real businesses do.

6. SEO starts before launch.
Not after.
Every page needs:
• Proper titles
• Meta descriptions
• H1 structure
• Schema
• Internal links
• Fast performance
Google rewards structure.

7. Stop guessing.
Install analytics.
Watch recordings.
Study heatmaps.
Track form abandonment.
The data already tells you why people aren't buying.

8. Your website isn't another marketing channel.
It's the destination for every marketing channel.
- SEO.
- LinkedIn.
- X.
- YouTube.
- - Email.
Ads.
Everything should point back to one place.
Your website.

A beautiful website is nice.
A website that generates leads every day is better.
Design for revenue.
Not awards.
