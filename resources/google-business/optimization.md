# Optimization

## Overview

Verification is only the first step.

A complete and regularly maintained Google Business Profile generally performs better than an incomplete profile.

Optimization should be treated as an ongoing process.

---

## Business Information

Complete all core information:

- Business name
- Primary category
- Secondary categories (if applicable)
- Phone number
- Website
- Business hours
- Service area
- Services

Keep information consistent across all online platforms.

---

## Business Description

A good business description should:

- Clearly explain what the business does.
- Describe the primary services.
- Mention the service area where appropriate.
- Be written naturally for customers.

Avoid keyword stuffing.

---

## Photos

Upload high-quality photos regularly.

Recommended photo types include:

- Logo
- Cover photo
- Team
- Equipment
- Vehicles
- Before & After projects
- Completed work

Photos help customers understand the business and build trust.

---

## Services

List the primary services provided.

Each service should have:

- Clear name
- Brief description
- Optional pricing if appropriate

Avoid adding services that are not actually offered.

---

## Reviews

Encourage genuine customer reviews.

Best practices:

- Ask satisfied customers politely.
- Respond professionally to all reviews.
- Never purchase fake reviews.

---

## Business Posts

Publish updates regularly.

Examples:

- Completed projects
- Promotions
- Seasonal services
- Company news

Regular updates help keep the profile active.

---

## Maintenance

Review the profile periodically.

Update whenever there are changes to:

- Hours
- Contact information
- Website
- Services
- Photos

Keeping information current improves customer experience.

---

## Best Practices

- Keep the profile complete.
- Maintain consistent information.
- Add new photos regularly.
- Respond to reviews promptly.
- Monitor profile performance.
- Keep improving over time.
