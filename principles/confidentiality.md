# Principle: Confidentiality

> **Client and employer confidentiality takes precedence over portfolio completeness.**
> Confidentiality is a fundamental professional responsibility. Client and employer information is treated as private by default and is never disclosed simply to make a portfolio, case study, or project appear more complete or impressive.

## Professional Commitment

Project work may involve access to information that is private, commercially sensitive, or intended only for internal use. Such information belongs to the client or employer and must be protected accordingly.
Confidential information may include, but is not limited to:

* Customer and user information
* Personal information and contact details
* Orders, transactions, and business data
* Internal processes and operational information
* Source code and proprietary implementations
* Credentials, API keys, and security-related information
* Internal documents, communications, and screenshots
* Pricing, financial information, and business strategies
* Unreleased products, features, or future plans
* Infrastructure and system details that should not be publicly exposed

## Portfolio and Case Studies

A portfolio only demonstrates **capability, judgment, and results** without compromising client or employer confidentiality.
Real project experience can be documented through appropriate technical descriptions, engineering decisions, challenges, and outcomes. However, confidential information should never be published merely because it would make a case study more detailed or visually compelling.
When appropriate, project documentation may use:

* Anonymized or generalized information
* Synthetic or reconstructed data
* Redacted screenshots
* Publicly available information
* High-level technical descriptions

## Disclosure Standard

Information should only be disclosed when it is clearly public, explicitly authorized for publication, or otherwise appropriate to share.
When the disclosure status of information is uncertain, the information should be omitted rather than exposed.
The standard is intentionally conservative:

> **When in doubt, protect the information first.**

## Guiding Principle

> **Trust is more valuable than portfolio completeness.**
> Some of the most valuable work may never be shown publicly. A portfolio does not need to reveal everything accomplished on a project to demonstrate professional capability.
> Protecting confidentiality is part of delivering professional-quality work—not a limitation of the portfolio.
